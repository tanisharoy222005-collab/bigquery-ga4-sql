# Dashboard KPIs

## Weekly Performance Metrics

The following KPIs are tracked weekly using GA4 + BigQuery for website performance reporting and stakeholder review.

### Traffic Metrics

- Sessions
- Users
- New users
- Returning users
- Engaged sessions
- Avg engagement time
- Bounce rate

---

### Acquisition Metrics

- Source / Medium
- Campaign performance
- Organic traffic
- Paid traffic
- Referral traffic
- Direct traffic

---

### Content Performance

- Top landing pages
- Page views
- Avg time on page
- Scroll depth
- Exit pages

---

### Conversion Metrics

- CTA clicks
- Form submissions
- Lead generation
- Conversion rate
- Assisted conversions

---

### CRM Reconciliation Metrics

- Total leads captured
- CRM matched leads
- GA4 tracked conversions
- Attribution mismatches
- Duplicate leads

---

## Reporting Purpose

Used for:

- Weekly stakeholder reporting
- Marketing performance reviews
- Campaign optimization
- Landing page performance analysis
- Lead quality validation
- Executive dashboard reporting

## Reporting Frequency

- Weekly snapshot
- Monthly trend review
- Quarterly performance comparison
