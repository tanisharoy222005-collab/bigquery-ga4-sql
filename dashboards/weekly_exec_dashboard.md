```md
# Weekly Executive Dashboard

## Overview

This dashboard summarizes weekly GA4 website performance and key acquisition/conversion trends.

Primary purpose:

- monitor traffic trends
- review campaign performance
- identify engagement opportunities
- validate lead generation performance
- share business insights with stakeholders

---

## Weekly KPIs

### Traffic

- Sessions
- Users
- New users
- Returning users
- Engaged sessions

---

### Engagement

- Avg engagement time
- Bounce rate
- Scroll depth
- Top landing pages
- Top content pages

---

### Acquisition

- Organic traffic
- Paid traffic
- Referral traffic
- Direct traffic
- Campaign source / medium

---

### Conversion

- CTA clicks
- Form submissions
- Leads generated
- Conversion rate
- Assisted conversions

---

### CRM Validation

- Total leads captured
- CRM matched leads
- Attribution mismatches
- Duplicate leads

---

## Audience

Shared with:

- Marketing team
- Business stakeholders
- Growth team
- Product stakeholders

---

## Reporting cadence

- Weekly snapshot
- Monthly performance review
- Quarterly trend analysis

---

## Notes

Metrics are validated through:

- GA4 reporting
- BigQuery exports
- CRM lead records
- UTM campaign tracking

Purpose:

Provide a clear weekly performance view and support decision-making across marketing and business teams.
```
